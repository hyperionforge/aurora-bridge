HOW TO PLAY

1. One phone taps HOST A NEW BRIDGE. The others open the app and tap the bridge in the list (or open the host's address in a phone browser - that works on iPhones too).

2. In CREW ASSEMBLY, decide whether you want a CAPTAIN. The ship's stations are shared out among the crew automatically: 1 crew = everything, 2 = Helm & Science / Tactical & Engineering, 3 = Conn / Tactical & Science / Engineering, 4 = Helm / Science & Comms / Tactical / Engineering. Each player taps the station they want.

3. Pick a mission and difficulty, then START MISSION. Use the tabs down the left side of your phone. VIEWER (top right) shows the main viewer and changes what the whole bridge sees.

4. Talk to each other! Science finds the enemy's shield frequency, Tactical matches it, Engineering keeps the power and heat in check, Helm keeps the enemy in the phaser arc. The captain gets advice from the first officer and sends orders that pop up on the right crewmate's phone.

Host: keep the app open and the screen on during a mission - the game runs on your phone.
